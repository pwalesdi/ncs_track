MOC performance

Made the final = finished top 9 in the long jump, triple jump, shot put or discus, or top 8 in every other event, relays included. (The 800 and 1600 finals seat 12; we count top 8.)

Of entries that competed at the MOC, how many made the final, by Area and route. Entries are athlete-events: one athlete in two events counts twice.

Spot use

What happened to each Area's guaranteed spots in each event: its automatic spots (6; Class A 3) plus the next-best-mark spots it won. Spots are assigned after declarations: when a finisher declines, the spot passes down to the next finisher from that Area. A decline before the deadline is not a no-show and is not counted against the Area. Field size plays no part.

  • Competed: the qualifier ran the event at the MOC.
  • No-show: the qualifier was in the MOC program for the event but didn't compete in it (did not start, or absent from its results), even if they competed in other events that day.
  • Not used: no entrant from that Area took the spot.

Centre: share of guaranteed spots whose qualifier competed. At-large standard qualifiers have no fixed number of spots, so they are listed on a separate line under each donut and are not in the donut or the %.

No-shows, 2022–2026

Every MOC entry (any route) that was in the program but didn't compete in the event, by the Area it came from. Follows the gender and event filters; the season filter doesn't apply.

Left out

Left out = finished in an Area final behind her Area's last automatic qualifier (so she was never offered a spot), and is not in the MOC field by any route. For each, we count the automatic qualifiers from other Areas whose Area-final marks were slower. Only automatic qualifiers are compared: by design, nobody left out has a better mark than a next-best-mark qualifier.
