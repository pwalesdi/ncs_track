NCS Meet of Champions: who qualifies, and how

Replay of each season's qualifying rules against the Area results, 2022–2026. No athlete names. Data version __BUILT__. Every rate shows its count.

Headline figures: 2022–2026 pooled, all events, girls + boys (they don't follow the filters).

The core question

Do some Areas' last automatic qualifiers do worse at the MOC than other Areas' at-large qualifiers?

For each Area: its lowest automatic qualifiers (Area places 5–6; 3rd for Class A) against at-large qualifiers from the other three Areas, among athletes who competed at the MOC. Bars show the top-finish rate (MOC top 8; top 9 in LJ/TJ/SP/DT), with the median MOC place (lower is better). Most other Areas' at-large qualifiers are Tri-Valley athletes. Small counts swing a lot.
