# NCS Track & Field Qualification Rules — 2026 (source transcription)

Transcribed from the 2026 NCS documents (2026 MOC Guide, updated May 13 2026; 2026 Class A Track Guide; NCS area qualification sheets for Tri-Valley, Bay Shore, Redwood Empire; NBL advancement flyer dated 4/27/2026). Where documents disagree, both versions are recorded under "Discrepancies". Nothing here is inferred unless marked **[inference]**.

Scope: varsity individual events and relays. The 4x800 relay has its own rules (section 4).

---

## 1. League championship → Area / Class A meet

General rules (all areas):
- Only performances from **league championship finals** may be used to determine advancers.
- DQ'd competitors/relays at the league meet may not advance in that event.
- A league may only submit qualified entries, even if it doesn't fill its allotment.
- If a league fails to forward any entries in an individual event, its entries go to the other participating leagues. No league gets more than 6 entries. (Tri-Valley/Bay Shore sheets add: "the Meet Director must maintain equal entries from each participating league.")

## 2. Area / Class A meet → NCS Meet of Champions (MOC)

All events except 4x800:
| Meet | Auto entries |
|---|---|
| Tri-Valley | 6 |
| Redwood Empire | 6 |
| Bay Shore | 6 |
| Class A | 3 |
- Plus the **3 next best marks from the Area and Class A meets** → field of at least 24.
- Plus any athlete finishing outside the top 6 who meets or exceeds the **MOC at-large standard in the finals only** of their Area meet.
- Entries close 11:59 pm Sunday after the Area meets (May 17, 2026). Non-qualifying Area/Class A finalists may be advanced into vacancies created by scratches before the deadline.
- Scratches after the deadline are subject to the Honest Effort Rule.

## 3. MOC → CIF State

- Top 3 in each event final qualify.
- Non-top-3 finishers qualify by meeting the State at-large standard **in the final round** at the MOC (standards listed in the MOC guide as the at-large table; **[inference]** the table above serves both Area→MOC and MOC→State — the guide lists one table and references it for both).
- 4x800: top 2 only, no at-large.
- Unified: top 1 only.

## 4. 4x800 relay (separate system)

- No league qualification. Entries are based on verifiable FAT times from the current season (league meet times allowed if the league runs the event), on Athletic.net.
- Up to 16 entries per Area/Class A meet:
  - Class A: best 16 times from Class A schools
  - Redwood Empire: HDNL (Big 5) 2, MCAL 2, NBL 4, VVAL 2, + next best 6
  - Bay Shore: MVAL 2, TCAL 3, WACC 3, + next best 8
  - Tri-Valley: BVAL 2, DAL 3, EBAL 3, + next best 8
- Area → MOC: 2 per Area/Class A meet + next 8 best marks → field up to 16 (+ at-large per guide text).
- Excluded from the main allocation simulation.

## 5. Discrepancies and gaps (unresolved)

1. **MOC fill spots**: MOC Guide = 3 next best across all 4 meets. NBL flyer = "next 6 best marks from all 3 NCS Area Championships" (omits Class A). Treat MOC Guide as authoritative.
2. **Redwood Empire fill**: official sheet = "next 6–10"; NBL flyer = 10.
3. **League → Area at-large standards** for Tri-Valley, Bay Shore, Redwood Empire: referenced ("plus any at-large marks") but no standards table exists in our documents.
4. **Berean Christian's league**: "DAL" in list header, "DVAL" in text.
5. **"Equal entries" rule** (TV/Bay Shore) conflicts with 4/6/6 base split; probably applies only to redistribution.
6. **Wind**: no document states whether at-large marks must be wind-legal.
7. **Earlier seasons (2019, 2022–2025)**: rules for those years are not in hand; 2026 rules may not match.
