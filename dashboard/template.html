MOC performance

Made the final = finished top 9 in the long jump, triple jump, shot put or discus, or top 8 in every other event, relays included. (The 800 and 1600 finals seat 12; we count top 8.)

Of entries that competed at the MOC, how many made the final, by Area and route. Entries are athlete-events: one athlete in two events counts twice.

Spot use Unfilled: provisional — under verification

What happened to each guaranteed spot (automatic + next best mark; 24 per event). Each spot is counted once.

  • Competed: the qualifier ran the event at the MOC.
  • Refilled: the qualifier withdrew before the deadline and the next finalist from that Area took the spot.
  • Chose another event: the qualifier competed at the MOC, but in other events.
  • Didn't enter: the qualifier was not at the MOC at all.
  • Unfilled: nobody used the spot and the event field ended up short.

Centre: Guaranteed spots used = (competed + refilled) ÷ guaranteed spots. At-large standard qualifiers have no fixed number of spots, so they are listed on a separate line under each donut and are not in the donut or the %.
