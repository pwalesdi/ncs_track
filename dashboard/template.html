NCS Meet of Champions: who qualifies, and how

Built from the summary tables of the NCS qualification analysis (no athlete names). Data version: __BUILT__. Rates always show their counts.

1. Field makeup by Area

How to read this: each bar is one Area's share of the field, split by how its athletes got in. Automatic = top places at the Area meet; at-large = the 3 next-best-mark spots plus the at-large standard. Use the Field toggle to compare everyone who qualified with who actually declared.

2. At-large spots by Area

How to read this: of all at-large spots in the selection, the share each Area took, split into next best mark (fill spots) and at-large standard.

3. MOC performance

How to read this: of athletes who competed at the MOC, the share who reached a top finish (top 8; top 9 in LJ/TJ/SP/DT), by Area and how they qualified. Always the declared field. Small counts swing a lot; check the numbers.

4. Spot utilization

How to read this: of the spots each Area earned, how many were used. Empty lanes = spots that went to nobody (not used and not refilled); no-shows = declared but did not compete. Always the qualified field (spots earned).

5. Left-out athletes

How to read this: for each Area and event, the 3 best non-qualifiers by Area mark. The bar is how often their Area mark was at or better than that year's MOC top-8/9 mark. Different meets (wind, weather, competition): a comparison, not a prediction.

Flag list: repeated empty lanes

How to read this: Area × event combinations with empty lanes in 3 or more of the 5 seasons (filtered by gender and event; the season filter doesn't apply). Unused-spot counts are shown for comparison.
